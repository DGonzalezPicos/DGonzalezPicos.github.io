Research focus

High-resolution spectroscopy of exoplanets, brown dwarfs, and cool stars — composition, thermal structure, and isotope ratios as tracers of formation and chemical evolution.

About me

I am a PhD candidate at Leiden Observatory (2023–2027), interested in observational astronomy and exoplanet science. I enjoy developing tools for data reduction and analysis of high-resolution spectroscopy data. During my PhD, I have contributed to the ESO SupJup survey to disentangle the formation pathways of giant planets and brown dwarfs through isotopic ratios. I am also experienced in data reduction and analysis of JWST/NIRSpec observations of brown dwarfs. During my PhD, I have also extended our modelling efforts to fit the high-resolution spectra of M-dwarfs. Currently, I am working on both directly-imaged planets and M-dwarfs to push forward the understanding of their atmospheres and formation pathways.
